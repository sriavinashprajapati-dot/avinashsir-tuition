Privacy Policy

Application: Avinash Sir Tuition App (com.aistudio.upadertuition.vxmqtp)
Developer: Avinash Sir Tuition Classes, Gozariya, Gujarat, India
Last Updated: November 6, 2026

Avinash Sir Tuition Classes ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Avinash Sir Tuition App. This Privacy Policy informs students, parents, and guardians regarding our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data for users of our educational application.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum required information to provide students with syllabus-specific online tests and evaluate their academic performance:

  • Student Full Name: Used on test reports and student profile screens.
  • Username & PIN: Used for student login. PINs are salted and cryptographically hashed using SHA-256; plain text PINs are never stored or transmitted.
  • Class / Standard (5th to 10th): To show the relevant curriculum and tests.
  • Roll Number: To maintain class organization.
  • Test Performance Data: Score, total marks, percentage, elapsed time, and submission timestamp.

We do not collect GPS location, contacts, photos, camera/microphone data, advertising identifiers, or payment details.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To grant secure access to educational tests.
  • To calculate test results, provide instant feedback, and maintain score history.
  • To allow the tuition administrator (Avinash Sir) to track academic progress and provide targeted mentoring.
  • To synchronize test questions and results between student devices and the cloud database via Google Cloud / Firebase.

3. Data Storage & Security

Security Standards:
  • Data in Transit: All data transmitted between the Android application and Firebase Firestore is encrypted via TLS 1.3 / HTTPS.
  • Data at Rest: Local data is stored in the app's sandboxed SQLite (Room) database on the device. Cloud data is stored in secure Google Cloud Firestore infrastructure.
  • No Advertising or Tracking: The application does not contain advertising SDKs, analytics trackers, or user profiling tools.

4. Children's Privacy (Under 13)

Our app serves students in Standards 5 to 10 (ages 10 to 16). Because students may be under 13 years of age, we strictly comply with Google Play's Families Policy: no targeted ads, no tracking cookies, and strictly educational usage. Parents or guardians may review or delete their child's records at any time.

5. Account & Data Deletion Rights

Users have full control over their personal data:

  • In-App Deletion: Go to Student Dashboard → Settings → Delete Account (એકાઉન્ટ ડિલીટ). This immediately deletes the student record from both device and cloud database.
  • Web Deletion Request: Visit our Account Deletion Page or email sriavinashprajapati@gmail.com.
